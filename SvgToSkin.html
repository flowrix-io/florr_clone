Fit

Size 50 fills the flower body (radius 25). Anything past radius 100 in local space is clipped by the renderer.

Skins can't subtract shapes, so a donut or a cut-out has to be painted over — the default is the flower-body yellow. The CLI drops holes instead unless you pass --hole-fill.
